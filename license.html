Questions

Do I need a developer?
Someone comfortable creating Supabase and Vercel projects can follow the guide. No framework knowledge is needed, but changing features means editing JavaScript.
Is support included?
The license is sold as-is, without ongoing support. The setup guide (and the video walkthrough in Pro) covers installation. Pro includes 6 months of code updates.
Can I change the branding, language and prices?
Yes. Logo, colors, texts, plans and prices are yours to change. The interface ships in English and Indonesian, and more languages can be added in one dictionary file.
What am I not allowed to do?
The license is non-exclusive and covers one business or brand. You may not resell, share or publish the source code, or sell it as a template or white-label product to others.
How do I pay from outside Indonesia?
We send an invoice for international payment. Message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm the details.
Can I add my own jersey models?
Yes. Each model is a GLB file with UVs in pattern coordinates plus a DXF-AAMA pattern file. The admin dashboard has an import for new models.
